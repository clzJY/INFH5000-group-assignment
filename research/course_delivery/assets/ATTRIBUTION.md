# Visual sources

- `scene_X/S/SC.png`, `poster_X/S/SC.png`: original native MetaUrban simulator recordings from the completed project, not generated street photographs. Fixed illustration conditions are documented in the report and video manifest.
- `actual_training_input.png`: an actual model-training input; its recorded label/condition/hash appear in `training_input_identity.json`.
- `scene_atlas.png`, `pipeline.png`, `main_results.png`: project graphics prepared from recorded native scenes, declared methodology and audited JSON results. See `research/make_figures.py`.
- `hkust_logo.png`: the original logo image extracted from Ma Wanqin's [HKUST Lab Report Template](https://www.overleaf.com/latex/templates/hkust-lab-report-template/wypwcqnrmhqj), listed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The extracted image was reconstructed with its original alpha mask and transparent margins removed for layout. The university mark identifies the course report; no endorsement is claimed.

Other research projects supplied layout/argument references only; their figures, data and research content are not included here.
