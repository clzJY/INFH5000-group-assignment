# Protocol and execution identity

`protocol_final.json` fixes the scenario factors, condition seeds, action/time budgets and shared simulation/planning implementation. The five critical files are `run_native.py`, `routing.py`, `local_planner.py`, `observation.py` and `probe_scene.py`. Their hashes remain identical across all formal runs. Its additional helper/model hashes describe the development state at that source snapshot.

`protocol_model_v3.json` specifies the final vision/text adapter recipe and model-source identities. Model development continued on training/validation data while the independent classical campaign was running. The final seed-0 checkpoint was selected on validation and fixed before primary visual testing. Seeds 1/2 and the curve-excluded model were fixed before their own formal tests. No checkpoint or controller was selected using the intersection audit or the completed three-family outcomes.

The model revisions included a text-only adapter, a bfloat16 multimodal adapter, and the final FP32 multimodal adapters. These development variants differ in more than one factor and are not a controlled architecture ablation. The final comparison uses `vlm_policy_v3.py`, `train_vlm_v3.py` and `serve_v3.py`, together with their parent modules.

`protocol_v1.json` is a retained development record. Use the final core and model protocols together for reproduction. `audit_provenance.py` checks both identities instead of treating obsolete development-helper hashes as the current model recipe.

Additional descriptive hierarchy contrasts and seed-only uncertainty blocks were specified after the completed intersection audit and before complete three-family aggregation. They supplement the primary adapted-versus-rule comparison; they do not guide model selection.

Runtime changes moved temporary/model caches to memory and redistributed EGL renderers after verifying device identity. Fixed-condition probes retained all controls and physical trajectories; rendered edge/animation details and some probabilities differed. Checkpoint routing and fixed-input probabilities were checked separately. Reproduction should preserve physical conditions and acknowledge rendering variation.

Native initialization failures and an execution interruption are archived separately. Incomplete attempts are repeated at the same seed/method/checkpoint. Completed navigation failures, contacts and timeouts remain in the planned test denominators. The public execution record reports administrative recovery separately from completed formal outcomes; full operational evidence remains in the timestamped workspace storage records.
